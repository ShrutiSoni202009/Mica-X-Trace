Smart Protection Panel

A modular, mica-based battery insulation panel concept proposed for EV battery systems, initially focusing on e-rickshaws and electric scooters.

01 · Product overview

MICA-X uses multiple interlocking panel modules intended to adapt to different battery-pack layouts. If a panel module is damaged, the concept is to inspect and replace that panel module rather than discard the complete panel assembly. This does not mean replacing an overheated battery cell.

Interlocking modules

Smaller connected modules are proposed to support different panel layouts.

Module-level replacement

A damaged panel module may be individually inspected and replaced, subject to final design validation.

Visible colour-changing indicator

A thermochromic film is proposed on each module so a technician can identify a module showing a temperature-related colour change. The activation threshold and response must be selected and tested; it is not an electronic sensor.

02 · Proposed five-layer structure

These are the current concept-layer roles. Final material grades, thicknesses, bonding method and performance require engineering validation.

Layer 1 · Protective outer layerE-glass fibreglass fabric with a proposed silicone-rubber coating. Intended to provide a protective outer surface; exact fabric, coating grade and temperature rating must be selected and tested.
Layer 2 · Thermal indicator layerThermochromic temperature-sensitive film. It is intended to change colour when its selected temperature threshold is reached. The indicator must remain visible on the module surface or through a transparent viewing window; the threshold is not finalised.
Layer 3 · Main mica insulation layerPhlogopite mica sheet, proposed as the main electrical-insulation barrier and a heat-resistant layer. Thickness, grade and dielectric performance require testing.
Layer 4 · Thermal support layerCeramic-fibre paper, proposed as a heat-resistant support/insulating layer. It must be safely enclosed or encapsulated to prevent fibre release during handling and use.
Layer 5 · Structural mounting baseA heat-resistant structural base to support the module and its interlocking geometry. A suitable high-temperature glass-fibre composite may be evaluated, but ordinary epoxy/GFRP must not be assumed suitable without checking its glass-transition temperature and test performance.

Design clarification: The five-layer stack is a proposed concept, not a validated production specification. Mica and ceramic-fibre layers provide insulation; they do not actively cool the battery. Any separate cooling panel or heat-spreader shown in the physical model is a distinct subsystem whose material, position and heat-flow design still need to be finalised. Thermochromic colour change is a visual indication for inspection, not an electronic sensor or a replacement for the battery-management system.

03 · Proposed manufacturing process

1. Responsible sourcing

Procure mica and other materials from documented, legally compliant suppliers.

2. Cleaning & inspection

Clean, inspect and sort incoming materials against defined specifications.

3. Layer preparation

Prepare mica, support, indicator and protective layers to the selected design.

4. Layer assembly

Assemble the five layers using a validated heat-resistant process.

5. Module fabrication

Cut panels to size and form the interlocking module geometry.

6. Testing & traceability

Test insulation, thermal response, adhesion, durability and fit; assign product ID and QR label.

04 · Intended impact & sustainability

Adaptable panel layout

Modular dimensions are intended to support different battery geometries after fit validation.

Targeted panel maintenance

If inspection confirms that an individual panel module is damaged, the concept allows that panel module to be replaced instead of replacing the complete panel assembly. Battery-cell repair or replacement is a separate matter.

Local value addition

The project proposes developing more mica processing and manufacturing value within Jharkhand.

Safety note: MICA-X is an idea/prototype-stage concept, not a certified product. No claim of fire prevention, thermal-runaway prevention, universal compatibility or commercial readiness is made. These require independent engineering validation and applicable certification.
