Smart Protection Panel

A modular, mica-based battery insulation panel concept designed to support thermal protection in electric-vehicle battery systems.

Modular designReplaceable panel moduleVisual thermal indicator concept

01 · Product overview

MICA-X Smart Protection Panel (MX-SPP-001) is a proposed modular insulation system for EV battery applications, with an initial focus on e-rickshaws and electric scooters. Its interlocking modules are intended to adapt the panel layout to different battery-pack dimensions.

DESIGN

Interlocking modules

Multiple smaller modules are arranged to suit the battery layout rather than relying on one single large panel.

MAINTENANCE CONCEPT

Module-level replacement

If a panel module is damaged, the affected panel module may be replaced after inspection. This does not mean replacing an overheated battery cell.

INDICATION CONCEPT

Colour-changing indicator

A visual indicator is proposed to help identify a heat-affected area for technician inspection. It is not a substitute for a certified battery-management or fire-protection system.

03 · Proposed manufacturing flow

The intended workflow for a future legal, quality-controlled manufacturing unit.

1. Responsible sourcing

Obtain mica and other inputs from documented, legally compliant suppliers.

2. Inspection & preparation

Check incoming material quality and prepare it to the required specifications.

3. Layer fabrication

Prepare and assemble the specified five-layer panel structure.

4. Cutting & interlocking

Cut modules to design dimensions and form the interlocking edges.

5. Indicator integration

Fit the proposed visual thermal indicator and inspect its placement.

6. Quality & safety testing

Test insulation, temperature response, durability and fit before any real-world use.

04 · Intended benefits & sustainability

Adaptable fit

Modular sizing is intended to make panel layouts adaptable to different battery-pack geometries.

Targeted maintenance

Replaceable panel modules may reduce the need to discard an entire panel assembly when one module is damaged.

Local value addition

The project proposes developing more processing and manufacturing value within Jharkhand, subject to feasibility and compliance.

Safety note: MICA-X is an idea/prototype-stage concept. No claim of certified fire prevention, thermal-runaway prevention, universal battery compatibility or commercial readiness is made. These require independent engineering validation and applicable certification.
